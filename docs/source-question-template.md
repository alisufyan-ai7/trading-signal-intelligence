End users receive only our derived outputs such as signal direction, entry/invalidation/target zones, scoring, explanations and performance history. Is this use permitted under our account/API terms? If separate market-data, commercial, partnership or redistribution/derived-data authorization is required, please identify the applicable agreement and pricing/contact.
