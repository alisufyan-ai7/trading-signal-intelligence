# S03 Momentum Continuation — v0.1

**Status:** IDEA  
**Created:** 2026-09-16

## Hypothesis

After a measurable directional momentum expansion, continuation may have positive expectancy when higher-timeframe structure agrees and the move is not already statistically exhausted.

## Guardrails before implementation

- Define momentum with measurable returns/range features, not visual labels.
- Freeze exhaustion filters before broad tests.
- Test event-driven spikes separately.
- Include conservative slippage for fast markets.
- Compare against S02 to ensure the strategy contributes distinct behavior rather than duplicate signals.
