INDEPENDENT CONTROL • S02

A fixed benchmark for testing whether AI adds value

S02 is a frozen deterministic strategy running beside VowSig AI. It does not drive, train or inform AI decisions; it exists as an independent control so AI performance can be judged against a transparent rules-based baseline.

01 Fixed control

S02 rules stay frozen while forward evidence accumulates, preventing the benchmark from moving to flatter the AI.

02 Independent from AI

S02 signals, positions and results are not inputs to the AI decision process and are never pooled into AI validation gates.

03 Supporting evidence

The customer product is VowSig AI. S02 remains visible so anyone can inspect the non-AI baseline used for comparison.
