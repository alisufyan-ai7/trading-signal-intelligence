DECISION SEMANTICS

BUY, SELL and NO_TRADE

VowSig's AI decision space includes an explicit abstention state. NO_TRADE is not treated as an error.

BUY

A BUY decision means the AI protocol identified a long-direction setup that passed the deterministic publication guardrails for that inference. Any resulting signal remains a shadow record.

SELL

A SELL decision is the corresponding short-direction output under the same frozen protocol and guardrails.

NO_TRADE

NO_TRADE means the system abstains. It is a valid outcome when the AI does not identify a publishable setup or when deterministic rules prevent a signal from being published.

Why abstention matters

A system that can abstain does not need to force a trade every hour. VowSig records valid inferences and keeps NO_TRADE separate from operational misses or errors.
