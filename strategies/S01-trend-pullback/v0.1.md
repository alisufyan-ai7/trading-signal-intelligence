# S01 Trend Pullback — v0.1

**Status:** FROZEN BASELINE — NOT YET TESTED  
**Created:** 2026-09-16  
**Frozen:** 2026-09-17

## Hypothesis

When a market is in an established higher-timeframe trend, a controlled pullback toward a medium-term trend area followed by renewed lower-timeframe momentum has positive continuation expectancy after realistic costs.

This version is intentionally simple. It exists to establish a reproducible baseline, not to maximize backtest performance.

## Rule-freeze principle

The rules below are frozen **before** inspecting the first S01 backtest results. If this baseline fails, it remains recorded as a failed/rejected version. Any later rule changes require a new version and a new evaluation plan; v0.1 will not be silently rewritten to fit observed results.

## Timeframes

- execution / signal timeframe: H1
- regime timeframe: H4
- evaluate signals only on closed H1 bars
- only fully closed H4 information available at the H1 signal timestamp may be used

## Initial instruments

First baseline evidence priority:

1. EUR/USD
2. XAU/USD

Expansion after the engine is verified:

## Indicators

All indicator values use closed bars only.

### H4

- EMA fast: 50
- EMA slow: 200
- EMA-fast slope lookback: 3 completed H4 bars

### H1

- EMA pullback: 20
- EMA guard: 50
- ATR: 14 using Wilder-style true range smoothing or a clearly documented mathematically equivalent implementation

## H4 regime rule

### Long regime

All must be true on the most recently completed H4 bar available at signal time:

1. EMA50 > EMA200
2. EMA50 > EMA50 from 3 completed H4 bars earlier
3. H4 close > EMA200

### Short regime

Symmetric:

1. EMA50 < EMA200
2. EMA50 < EMA50 from 3 completed H4 bars earlier
3. H4 close < EMA200

No additional ADX, RSI, news, session or discretionary regime filters exist in v0.1.

## H1 pullback rule

The three completed H1 bars immediately **before** the signal candle form the pullback window.

For each bar define normalized EMA20 distance using that bar's own indicator values.

### Long pullback

Within the prior three bars:

- at least one bar has `low <= EMA20 + 0.25 * ATR14`;
- no bar has `low < EMA50 - 0.50 * ATR14`.

### Short pullback

Within the prior three bars:

- at least one bar has `high >= EMA20 - 0.25 * ATR14`;
- no bar has `high > EMA50 + 0.50 * ATR14`.

The guard is deliberately simple: a pullback may probe around EMA20 but should not become a deep H1 trend failure.

## H1 momentum re-entry signal

### Long signal candle

All must be true:

1. long H4 regime is active;
2. long pullback rule is satisfied by the prior three H1 bars;
3. signal candle closes above its open;
4. signal candle closes above H1 EMA20;
5. signal candle close > previous H1 bar high.

### Short signal candle

Symmetric:

1. short H4 regime is active;
2. short pullback rule is satisfied by the prior three H1 bars;
3. signal candle closes below its open;
4. signal candle closes below H1 EMA20;
5. signal candle close < previous H1 bar low.

## Entry

- Enter at the **next H1 bar open** after the signal candle.
- If there is no next bar, there is no trade.
- No same-candle entry is permitted.
- One position per instrument at a time in v0.1; ignore additional signals while a trade is open.

## Stop

Use a fixed ATR stop for this baseline:

- stop distance = `1.5 * ATR14` measured from the next-bar entry open
- ATR value is the value known at the signal candle close

Long: stop below entry.  
Short: stop above entry.

No structural-stop alternative is mixed into v0.1.

## Target

- fixed target = 2.0R gross price movement from entry
- no partial exits
- no trailing stop

## Time stop

If neither target nor stop has been reached after 48 H1 bars following entry, exit at the 48th bar close.

## Intrabar ambiguity policy

The H1 backtest does not know tick sequencing inside a candle.

Conservative rules:

1. if a bar gaps through the stop at its open, exit at that bar open;
2. if a bar opens beyond the target, credit no better than the target price;
3. if both stop and target are touched within the same bar and the open does not resolve the sequence, **assume the stop was hit first**;
4. no optimistic intrabar ordering is allowed.

## Baseline cost model

MT5 OHLC bars and their historical `spread` field are not treated as perfect executable bid/ask history.

For the first alpha baseline:

- preserve the historical spread field in broker points;
- convert spread points to price using source symbol point size;
- effective transaction cost = `1.25 * entry-bar spread price`;
- if entry-bar spread is zero/missing, use the trailing 20-bar median non-zero spread available before entry;
- if no valid historical spread estimate exists, skip/flag the trade rather than assume zero cost;
- net P&L = gross price P&L minus effective transaction cost;
- report both gross R and net R.

The 1.25 multiplier represents one observed-spread charge plus a simple 25% buffer for slippage/model uncertainty. Later experiments must include 1.0x, 1.5x and 2.0x cost stress without rewriting this baseline.

For Binance crypto expansion, exchange fee/slippage assumptions will be specified separately before those results are evaluated; MT5 CFD spread rules must not be silently applied to Binance Spot.

## Position sizing / metrics

Strategy evidence is primarily expressed in R-multiples rather than account dollars.

For every trade record at minimum:

- signal timestamp
- entry timestamp
- direction
- entry
- stop
- target
- ATR
- spread points / price cost
- exit timestamp / price / reason
- gross R
- net R
- bars held

## Fixed research splits for initial MT5 H1/H4 evidence

Use calendar splits rather than choosing dates after seeing results:

- **development/train:** available history through 2022-12-31
- **validation:** 2023-01-01 through 2024-12-31
- **final test:** 2025-01-01 onward

Warm-up bars needed for EMA200 are not counted as evaluation observations.

The final-test period must not be used to select v0.1 parameters.

## Evaluation protocol

Report at minimum:

- trade count
- win rate
- gross and net expectancy in R
- median net R
- profit factor
- maximum cumulative-R drawdown
- long vs short results
- yearly breakdown
- instrument breakdown
- development / validation / final-test breakdown
- cost stress results

After the fixed baseline run:

- walk-forward analysis may begin;
- parameter-neighborhood tests may vary one declared parameter at a time;
- no profitable neighbor retroactively replaces this v0.1 baseline.

## Decision rule

v0.1 becomes `CANDIDATE` only if it demonstrates positive **after-cost** expectancy outside the development sample, acceptable drawdown and sample size, and no obvious dependence on a single instrument/year.

A high win rate alone is insufficient. A failed baseline is recorded as `REJECTED` or `NEEDS NEW HYPOTHESIS`; it is not cosmetically optimized in place.
