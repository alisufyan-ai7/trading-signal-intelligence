# S04 Mean Reversion — v0.1

**Status:** IDEA  
**Created:** 2026-09-16

## Hypothesis

In objectively non-trending regimes, statistically stretched price movement away from a short/medium-term equilibrium may revert sufficiently to produce positive expectancy after costs.

## Guardrails before implementation

- Require an explicit non-trend/regime filter.
- Define stretch in normalized terms (for example ATR/z-score families) before testing.
- Avoid averaging down or martingale logic.
- Use a hard invalidation/stop.
- Analyze event/news periods and persistent trends as expected failure modes.
