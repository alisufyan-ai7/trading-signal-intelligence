# AI-0 — Product naming

_Status: WORKING NAME SELECTED — 2026-09-28_

## Working product name

# SignalProof AI

**Working descriptor:** AI market intelligence with a verifiable record.

**Working promise:**

> AI finds the setup. Risk rules verify it. Every signal stays on the record.

## Why this name

The name intentionally combines the two product ideas that should remain inseparable:

- **Signal** — the user comes for actionable market intelligence;
- **Proof** — every published signal should have a durable, attributable outcome record rather than disappearing after issuance.

This differentiates the project from a generic "AI predicts markets" product while still allowing an AI-first user experience.

## Naming boundary

`SignalProof AI` is a **working product name**, not a representation that trademark/domain clearance has been completed.

Under the project's strict context-isolation rule, no broad external brand/trademark/domain search was performed here. Before a paid public launch, perform a separate clearance check and either confirm this name or replace it.

## Immediate implementation consequence

Visible product copy should stop using the competitor brand `TradingTop` for this application's own identity.

Internal repository/package names such as `trading-signal-intelligence` may remain unchanged because they are engineering identifiers rather than the customer-facing brand.

The existing Railway URL/service name may remain temporarily during the engineering transition; a distinct public hostname should be created before broader external promotion.

## Product language

Preferred:

- AI market intelligence
- AI inference
- deterministic risk guardrails
- verified / immutable record
- model-specific evidence
- experimental / shadow AI where applicable

Avoid:

- guaranteed accuracy
- guaranteed profit
- "AI knows where price will go"
- claiming confidence is a calibrated probability before calibration exists
- describing the active S02 feed as AI-generated

## Current public-feed disclosure

Until AI-v1 is activated and separately validated, the current public S02 signal feed remains rule-based.

The new brand does **not** change the provenance of existing signals.
