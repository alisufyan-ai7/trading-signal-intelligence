## Hypothesis

A close-confirmed H1 breakout beyond objectively defined recent structure can exhibit continuation expectancy after costs when the breakout occurs in the direction of the broader H4 regime and is accompanied by volatility and tick-volume expansion.

This version is frozen before any S02 result is viewed. Do not modify v0.1 after the first result; any change becomes a new version.

## Intended regime

Designed for directional/trending periods with expanding participation.

Expected to struggle in:

- range-bound/choppy markets;
- false-breakout regimes;
- event gaps that open beyond structure;
- extremely wide-spread or illiquid conditions.

## Initial instruments / timeframes

Predeclared first research universe:

- EUR/USD
- GBP/USD
- USD/JPY
- XAU/USD

Execution/signal timeframe: **H1**.  
Regime timeframe: **H4**.

Do not remove a weak market from this initial baseline after results are seen.

## Features

All features use closed candles only.

### H1 structure

For H1 signal bar `t`:

- `upper_structure[t]` = maximum H1 high over the **20 completed bars immediately before t**;
- `lower_structure[t]` = minimum H1 low over the **20 completed bars immediately before t**.

The signal bar itself is excluded from the structure window.

### H1 ATR

- ATR14 using Wilder-style exponential smoothing (`alpha = 1/14`).

### Breakout distance

Long breakout requires:

`close[t] > upper_structure[t] + 0.10 * ATR14[t]`

Short breakout requires:

`close[t] < lower_structure[t] - 0.10 * ATR14[t]`

### Gap-open exclusion

To separate a close-confirmed intrabar breakout from a bar that already opened beyond structure:

- long: `open[t] <= upper_structure[t]`;
- short: `open[t] >= lower_structure[t]`.

A signal bar that opens already beyond the relevant structure is excluded from v0.1.

### Volatility expansion

Define H1 true range in the standard way from high/low/previous close.

Signal-bar true range must be at least **1.25×** the median true range of the **20 prior completed H1 bars**.

### Participation expansion

Signal-bar tick volume must be at least **1.10×** the median tick volume of the **20 prior completed H1 bars**.

### H4 regime

Compute H4 EMA50 on closed H4 candles. An H4 candle is available to an H1 decision only after its four-hour close.

Long regime:

- latest available H4 close > H4 EMA50; and
- H4 EMA50 > its value 3 H4 bars earlier.

Short regime:

- latest available H4 close < H4 EMA50; and
- H4 EMA50 < its value 3 H4 bars earlier.

No H4 EMA200 filter is used in S02 v0.1.

## Entry

Evaluate signals only after an H1 candle closes.

Long signal requires all of:

1. long H4 regime;
2. close-confirmed long structure breakout;
3. gap-open exclusion passes;
4. signal candle closes above its open;
5. volatility expansion passes;
6. tick-volume expansion passes.

Short conditions are symmetric.

Entry is the **next stored H1 bar open**.

Only one position per instrument may be open at a time.

## Stop / invalidation

Risk distance = **1.75 × signal-bar ATR14**.

- long stop = entry − risk distance;
- short stop = entry + risk distance.

No intratrade stop movement in v0.1.

## Exit / targets

Fixed target = **2.5R** from entry.

Maximum holding period = **72 H1 bars**.

If neither stop nor target is hit by the end of the 72nd entry/holding bar, exit at that bar's close.

If stop and target are both touched within the same H1 bar and intrabar order is unknowable, resolve pessimistically to **stop first**.

Gap behavior:

- if a subsequent bar opens beyond the stop, fill at that bar open (`gap_stop`);
- if a subsequent bar opens beyond the target, cap the favorable fill at the target (`gap_target`).

## Costs / execution model

Baseline transaction-cost proxy:

- use the completed **signal-bar historical MT5 spread**;
- if signal spread is zero/missing, use the median non-zero spread of the prior 20 H1 bars only;
- cost price = `1.25 × known_spread_points × point_size`;
- subtract this cost once from gross trade P&L before converting to net R.

The 1.25 multiplier is an execution-model convention carried across baseline strategies, not an S02 signal parameter.

No claim is made that MT5 bar spread equals exact executable historical bid/ask. Later robustness testing must stress costs.

## Position sizing

Backtest performance is expressed in R-multiples. No compounding or account-size assumptions are part of signal-alpha evaluation.

## Evaluation protocol

Use the same fixed temporal splits for comparability:

- development: signal timestamp before 2023-01-01 UTC;
- validation: 2023-01-01 through 2024-12-31 UTC;
- final test: 2025-01-01 onward.

These dates have been observed in S01 research, so they are not globally pristine market history. However, S02 rules/parameters are frozen before viewing any S02 outcomes, which preserves an honest first S02 baseline. Strong claims still require new cross-market and forward evidence.

Report at minimum:

- trade count;
- win rate;
- gross and net expectancy R;
- profit factor;
- maximum cumulative-R drawdown;
- long/short counts;
- stop/target/time-stop counts;
- per-year and per-split results.

Before candidate promotion also require:

- candle-level implementation audit;
- cost stress;
- parameter-neighborhood sensitivity;
- walk-forward analysis;
- cross-market evidence;
- immutable forward testing.

## Promotion / rejection discipline

v0.1 is not promoted because of one strong market or one strong year.

A market may become a **candidate lead** only if both validation and final-test net expectancy are positive with adequate sample size and the result survives cost/parameter robustness checks.

A multi-market S02 candidate requires positive after-cost OOS evidence on more than one predeclared instrument without one market dominating the aggregate result.

If the baseline is broadly negative across validation/final-test periods, retain it as a failed benchmark rather than tuning v0.1 in place.

## Known failure modes

- false breakouts in ranges;
- breakout after scheduled/event volatility that is not repeatable;
- session gaps and discontinuities;
- tick-volume behavior that differs materially by source;
- spread widening around breakouts;
- H1 bars hiding intrabar stop/target sequence.
