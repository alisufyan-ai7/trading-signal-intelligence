## Frozen fee assumption

For every AI-v1.3 shadow signal:

`fee_rate_per_side = 0.001`

Equivalent:

`0.100% per side`

Frozen execution-role assumption:

- entry = taker;
- exit = taker.

Static round-trip fee fraction proxy:

`0.002 * reference_midpoint`

This is intentionally conservative relative to any future account that happens to
qualify for a lower fee.

Temporary fee promotions may not improve research results.

## Frozen spread assumption

The prospective Binance Spot top-of-book observation supplies:

- best bid;
- best ask;
- midpoint;
- spread price.

The static round-trip spread-crossing component is:

`1.0 * reference_spread`

Interpretation:

- one half-spread on entry;
- one half-spread on exit;
- one full spread in total.

The same static entry-time spread is used for the research proxy so later market
conditions cannot retroactively alter the cost estimate attached to a signal.

## Frozen slippage assumption

Binance does not provide a universal fixed slippage rate because realized
slippage depends on order size, book depth and market conditions.

AI-v1.3 currently has no admitted order-size / market-impact model.

Therefore the protocol preregisters a conservative, size-agnostic research
buffer:

`slippage_buffer = 1.0 * reference_spread`

This is **not** a Binance fee or Binance factual claim.

It is a protocol-owned modeling assumption chosen prospectively before any
performance-bearing crypto inference.

The buffer may not be reduced after activation based on observed AI outcomes.

## Frozen all-in static cost rule

At signal creation, freeze:

`reference_midpoint = (best_bid + best_ask) / 2`

`reference_spread = best_ask - best_bid`

Then:

`fee_component = 0.002 * reference_midpoint`

`spread_crossing_component = 1.0 * reference_spread`

`slippage_buffer_component = 1.0 * reference_spread`

and:

`static_round_trip_cost_price =
    0.002 * reference_midpoint + 2.0 * reference_spread`

The static cost is allocated pro rata across partial and final exits.

## Frozen cost guard

Carry forward the existing AI-v1 guard without tuning it against crypto
performance:

`max_round_trip_cost_r = 0.15`

For a proposed directional signal:

`risk_price = stop_atr * atr_14`

`estimated_cost_r = static_round_trip_cost_price / risk_price`

Reject the proposal with:

`cost_too_high`

when:

`estimated_cost_r > 0.15`

The 0.15R threshold is not selected from crypto outcomes.

It is carried forward to avoid optimizing a new threshold against the prospective
crypto cohort.

## BUY / SELL interpretation

The AI-v1.0 decision contract includes BUY and SELL.

Binance Spot does not imply that a naked directional short is executable merely
because a SELL research signal exists.

Therefore:

- BUY and SELL use the same symmetric shadow friction proxy;
- SELL is a directional research outcome only;
- the cost model does not claim Spot short execution;
- margin borrow interest is not included;
- perpetual-futures funding is not included;
- broker/exchange execution remains disabled.

Any future executable crypto venue/shorting design requires a separate execution
protocol and fee/funding model.

## Promotions and future Binance fee changes

The frozen research policy intentionally ignores temporary fee discounts.

If Binance later raises its standard non-discounted Regular User taker fee above
0.100%, the runtime must fail closed for new signals until a prospectively
versioned cost-policy correction is reviewed.

If Binance later lowers the fee or offers a promotion, AI-v1.3 does not
automatically reduce its frozen research cost.

This asymmetry prevents favorable fee changes from improving historical or
prospective research results without governance.
