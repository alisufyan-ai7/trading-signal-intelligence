AUDITABILITY

Why losses and unresolved outcomes stay visible

A trustworthy forward record should not contain only the convenient outcomes.

VowSig's forward evidence uses append-only, hash-linked records. The purpose is to make the sequence of activation, inference, signal and lifecycle events auditable rather than editable after the fact.

What stays in the record

Issued signals remain part of the record whether they later win, lose, remain open or close for another lifecycle reason. Operational misses and other recorded states are also not silently rewritten into successful inferences.

Why this matters

Selective deletion would make a forward record difficult to evaluate. Keeping unfavorable and unresolved outcomes visible helps separate product presentation from evidence preservation.
