# S05 Volatility Expansion — v0.1

**Status:** IDEA  
**Created:** 2026-09-16

## Hypothesis

Periods of objectively measured volatility compression can precede directional expansion; entering only after confirmed range expansion may produce positive expectancy when execution costs and false breakouts are controlled.

## Guardrails before implementation

- Define compression using fixed measurable statistics.
- Avoid selecting the breakout direction with future information.
- Test close-confirmed entry first.
- Measure false-breakout frequency and post-breakout MAE.
- Include spread/slippage sensitivity, especially around session opens and events.
