How VowSig separates evidence

VowSig keeps different evidence streams distinct so historical research, rule-based benchmarks and AI shadow results are not presented as the same thing.

AI shadow evidence

The active AI runtime is AI-v1.1. Its frozen decision protocol remains AI-v1.0. Decisions are collected in SHADOW_ONLY mode with broker execution disabled.

S02 benchmark

S02 is the project's separate deterministic rule-based research benchmark. Its evidence should not be relabeled as AI evidence, and AI results should not be merged into S02's record.

Historical vs forward

Historical backtests answer different questions from forward evidence. VowSig keeps the two visibly separated and does not treat historical performance as a guarantee.

Immutable forward records

Forward activation and subsequent events are preserved in hash-linked ledgers. Public summaries are derived from that evidence while raw broker data and hidden model reasoning remain outside the customer surface.

Validation thresholds

AI A1/A2/A3 and S02 forward gates are preregistered checkpoints. Early observations do not automatically promote a strategy or validate profitability.
