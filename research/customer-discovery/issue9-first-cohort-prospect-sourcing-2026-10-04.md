## Method, evidence and grading

Only this chat, the specified private repository and fresh publicly accessible web pages were used. Searches covered Gold/XAUUSD, Nasdaq/NQ/US100/USTEC and Dow/US30 technical discussion, original charts, entries, exits, risk, trade planning and analysis workflows. No other chats, account memory, unrelated connected data, private groups or lead lists were used.

Each retained row links a canonical public profile and at least one public original post or comment supporting market relevance. Thread-page links may contain several authors: the notes identify the relevant post numbers where available. Additional evidence links support multi-market classification where the principal linked page covers one market.

The recent-activity screen used original relevant discussion within approximately 90 days of 2026-10-04, with profile context considered as supporting evidence. Dates record the reviewed relevant contribution, not last login, latest post across all history, or a verified trade execution. All 28 Forex Factory rows carry visible calendar dates. The two Reddit rows retain the visible relative label `2mo ago (observed 2026-10-04)`; exact publication dates were not exposed in the retrieved public view.

Grade A indicates richer original, supported-market analysis or practical workflow evaluation with recent participation. Grade B indicates supported relevance with shorter, older or less extensive evidence. Grade is an internal sourcing judgment, not proof of competence, willingness to participate, trading performance or ability to pay. No C-grade rows were used to pad the pool.

Screening excluded visible promotion-oriented/organizational accounts, weak evidence, weak recency and explicit contact preferences. No reviewed retained evidence visibly indicated a minor or an explicit refusal of research/promotional contact. Age, identity and the completeness of public history are not verified. Ordinary public trading participation, rather than follower count or bio alone, supports inclusion.
