Tradebise's supported funnel inference combines market/editorial discoverability with registration, advertising and paid analysis/alerts. It remains an inference without traffic or conversion attribution. Its [promotion page](https://tradebise.com/promotion) sells discoverability while claiming algorithm independence; neither commercial effectiveness nor independence was independently tested. WaqarZaka's referral deposits are not subscription revenue; actual affiliate payout economics remain unknown.

Tradebise now has browser-confirmed open, target-hit and stop-loss samples. Its public Dataset definition counts signals that have not hit stop-loss, including ongoing calls, as success; it is not a completed-trade win rate. At capture 1, Dataset total 77,630 and stops 26,308 imply 66.11%, while markup declares 93% and visible total is 378,213. Stale/versioned metadata may explain this, but the population is unreconciled. Current WAIT/bullish outlook and historical open/closed call are distinct states. None of these observations establishes profitability or superiority.

TradingTop's earlier aggregate AI page reported 74% accuracy and expected return +31.86. Separate three-month model views showed T1: 41 trades, +$115.66, 56.1%; T2: 53 trades, -$441.30, 37.7%; T3: 30 trades, +$143.50, 70%. Those different scopes cannot be merged into one audited profitability claim. The losing model is visible evidence, while cost basis, completeness and calibration remain unknown.

TradingTop's observed tiers were Trial $0/300 TOP once, Access $69/month/5,000 TOP monthly, Advanced $199/quarter/10,000 TOP monthly, Pro $399/year/20,000 TOP monthly and Elite $1,599/lifetime/50,000 TOP monthly. Manual gold/BTC/index calls cost 100 TOP, other calls 50, T1/T2 access 200 TOP/day; agency tiers advertised 20–40%. These are historical observed commercial mechanics, not VowSig pricing recommendations.
