FORWARD VALIDATION

What shadow validation means

VowSig can record decisions and outcomes without sending broker orders.

SHADOW_ONLY means the active AI runtime produces and records research decisions while broker execution remains disabled.

Why use shadow validation?

It allows the project to accumulate untouched forward evidence before making later publishability or commercialization decisions. The protocol is not tuned merely because early results look good or bad.

Validation gates

The AI track uses preregistered A1, A2 and A3 evidence thresholds. Reaching a threshold triggers the review defined for that gate; it does not retroactively change earlier records.

Shadow validation is evidence collection, not live auto-trading.
