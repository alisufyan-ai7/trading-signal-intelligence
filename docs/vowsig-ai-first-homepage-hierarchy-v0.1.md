# VowSig AI-First Homepage Hierarchy v0.1

_Status: isolated candidate / not approved for production publication_  
_Tracker: GitHub Issue #38_

## Objective

Make the public VowSig homepage unmistakably AI-first while preserving S02 as a
transparent, independent deterministic benchmark.

The product hierarchy is:

1. **VowSig AI** — primary customer product and primary homepage story.
2. **Core AI-v1.1 shadow intelligence** — public decisions, outcomes and
   evidence from XAU/USD, NASDAQ 100 and DOW 30.
3. **AI-v1.2.1 expansion validation** — separate aggregate validation progress
   for EUR/USD, XAG/USD and WTI, with public actionable signal details disabled.
4. **S02 deterministic benchmark** — supporting validation/control evidence,
   not a co-equal product or an AI input.

## Why S02 remains

S02 remains valuable precisely because it is non-AI and frozen.

It provides an independent control for asking whether VowSig AI adds value over
a transparent rules-based baseline. S02 decisions, positions and results do not
drive, train or inform AI decisions and are not pooled into AI evidence gates.

Removing S02 from the research system would weaken future AI-vs-baseline
comparison. Presenting it as a co-equal customer product, however, creates
positioning ambiguity.

## Homepage hierarchy

### Above the fold

The hero now states that VowSig is an AI-first research beta and routes the
visitor first to:

- AI intelligence;
- AI validation/evidence.

The top status card is AI-first. Snapshot freshness remains visible and
benchmark degradation can still surface as a supporting warning.

### AI evidence

Core AI intelligence remains first and retains:

- AI runtime health;
- aggregate AI evidence;
- market-level forward collection;
- decision summaries;
- core shadow signal outcomes.

AI validation programs remain immediately after the core AI evidence so core
and expansion lineages are visible as separate governed programs.

### S02 benchmark

The prior four prominent S02 homepage sections are consolidated into one
supporting **Independent Control — S02** section.

The public explanation makes three points explicit:

1. S02 is a fixed control;
2. S02 is independent from AI;
3. S02 is supporting evidence, while VowSig AI is the product.

All existing S02 public evidence remains available inside an expandable
benchmark evidence area:

- forward benchmark markets;
- historical benchmark evidence;
- recent immutable benchmark signals;
- transfer-research markets.

No S02 record or public evidence surface is deleted.

## Evidence boundaries

The hierarchy must continue to distinguish:

- Core AI-v1.1 evidence;
- AI-v1.2.1 expansion evidence;
- S02 deterministic benchmark evidence;
- historical evidence vs prospective forward evidence.

Overlapping market symbols do not imply pooled evidence.

## Hard boundaries

This milestone does not authorize or change:

- AI-v1.1 or AI-v1.2.1 model/provider/prompt/policy/features/guardrails;
- AI decision or lifecycle semantics;
- S02 strategy rules or F1/F2/F3 gates;
- any ledger, evidence record or historical result;
- scheduler definitions or cadence;
- broker execution;
- AI-v1.2.1 actionable public signal exposure;
- backfill;
- unsupported AI-superiority/performance claims.

## Governance

The implementation changes activation-bound public-surface artifacts.

Before production publication it requires:

1. focused/static tests;
2. applicable full regression on the owner VPS;
3. a reviewed successor public-surface correction in the existing AI-v1.1
   correction chain;
4. exact candidate freeze;
5. owner approval for publication;
6. production cutover and hosted verification.

No production merge or deployment is authorized by this document.
