# EXP-2026-015 — AI-v1.2 parallel market-expansion preregistration

_Status: PREPARATION ONLY / NOT ACTIVATED — 2026-10-06_

## Research objective

Begin prospective shadow evidence collection on additional market families now,
without changing or contaminating the active AI-v1.1 XAUUSD / USTEC / US30
A2/A3 evidence lineage.

The expansion exists to make productive use of the same calendar period that
AI-v1.1 must spend accumulating time-diverse evidence.

## Frozen first expansion universe

1. EURUSD — EUR/USD — FX
2. XAGUSD — XAG/USD — metal
3. USOIL — WTI — energy

No market may be dropped from this first expansion set after performance-bearing
AI-v1.2 evidence is observed merely because its results are unfavorable.

## Why these markets

### EURUSD

- adds a major FX regime absent from active AI-v1.1;
- canonical Exness mapping already exists;
- EXP-2026-006 explicitly established about eight years of clean H1 research
  history, strictly increasing timestamps, no duplicates, populated tick volume
  and instrument-specific spread observations.

### XAGUSD

- adds a second metal and tests whether behavior seen on gold transfers to a
  related but distinct market;
- canonical Exness mapping already exists;
- it participated in the frozen S02 transfer experiment, providing existing
  mechanical/data-path experience;
- prior S02 transfer weakness is not a reason to exclude it from the AI
  experiment, because the AI protocol is a separate research hypothesis;
- live H1/H4/readiness/cost compatibility must be explicitly verified before
  activation.

### USOIL

- adds an energy regime absent from active AI-v1.1;
- canonical Exness mapping exists;
- EXP-2026-006 explicitly established about 8.4 years of clean H1 research
  history with populated spread/tick-volume fields;
- prior S02 weakness is retained as independent counterevidence rather than
  used to tune or exclude the market.

## Deferred markets

### GBPUSD and USDJPY

They remain eligible future expansion candidates, but are deliberately deferred
from the first expansion cohort. EURUSD is sufficient to prove the FX path
without adding three correlated FX markets at once.

### US500

It remains a valid product/research market but is deferred because active
AI-v1.1 already contains USTEC and US30. Adding US500 now contributes less
independent regime diversity than FX / silver / energy.

### Crypto

Crypto is excluded from this expansion. Binance/crypto source, session,
volume and execution-cost semantics differ from the MT5 protocol and require
their own prospective specification.

## Pre-activation compatibility gates

For each of EURUSD, XAGUSD and USOIL, verify prospectively before activation:

1. canonical source mapping is exact;
2. symbol is available in the intended MT5 environment;
3. point-size/digits are correct;
4. latest tick / H1 / H4 readiness semantics work under the existing bounded
   synchronization model;
5. completed-bar feature construction succeeds without market-specific
   semantic ambiguity;
6. required history windows are available;
7. spread/cost conversion is finite and instrument-specific;
8. session breaks fail closed rather than fabricating bars;
9. no provider call occurs during readiness/preflight checks;
10. no performance ledger is written during readiness/preflight checks.

If any market fails compatibility, preserve that failure as evidence. Do not
silently substitute a different market after observing a performance result.

A market may be removed before activation only for an objective pre-activation
technical incompatibility, with the reason durably recorded before any
AI-v1.2 performance evidence exists.

## Evaluation principles

The expansion is not allowed to shorten or weaken AI-v1.1 A2/A3.

The expansion calendar starts only at its own activation.

The purpose is independent breadth/generalization evidence, not a mechanism to
inflate the current AI-v1.1 sample size.

At every review:

- report aggregate and per-market results;
- never hide a weak market;
- preserve instrument-specific costs;
- distinguish operational misses from no-current-tick availability events;
- do not optimize from early outcomes;
- do not treat raw model confidence as probability.

Formal AI-v1.2 review thresholds will be frozen before activation after
compatibility/readiness work establishes expected opportunity/trade rates.
They must be fixed before the first performance-bearing inference.

## 2026-10-06 compatibility checkpoint — market allowlist requires version-isolated schema profile

Pre-activation review found that the frozen `config/ai_v1_feature_schema.json`
enumerates only XAUUSD / USTEC / US30 in `source_symbol`. The existing frozen
guardrail artifact likewise retains those three symbols as historical
`shadow_markets` metadata.

This was discovered before any AI-v1.2 performance-bearing inference.

Resolution:

- keep runtime/evidence identity `AI-v1.2`;
- keep decision protocol `AI-v1.0`;
- do not modify any active AI-v1.1 or frozen AI-v1.0 artifact;
- add a separate AI-v1.2 feature-schema validation profile whose feature
  semantics remain AI-v1.0 and whose only semantic compatibility change is the
  source-symbol allowlist;
- continue using the exact frozen AI-v1.0 policy, decision schema, provider
  parameters, guard numerical semantics, feature formulas, lifecycle and cost
  semantics;
- bind the expansion universe separately in
  `config/ai_v1_2_expansion.json`.

The compatibility verifier must mechanically prove that the expansion feature
schema equals the frozen base feature schema after normalizing only schema
ID/title and the source-symbol allowlist.

No activation authority is created by this checkpoint.
