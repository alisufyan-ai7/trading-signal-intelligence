SIGNAL LIFECYCLE

Entry, stop, targets and expiry

A signal is more than a BUY or SELL label. Its risk levels and lifecycle determine how the public record is interpreted.

Entry

The entry level is the reference price attached to an issued shadow signal.

Stop

The stop defines the adverse-price boundary used by the recorded lifecycle and risk framing.

TP1 and TP2

Targets represent predefined favorable-price milestones. A partial target and a final target may produce different lifecycle events.

Expiry and closure

A signal may also expire or close because of its frozen lifecycle rules. VowSig records the resulting state rather than presenting only profitable exits.

The public lifecycle is descriptive research evidence. It is not a personalized position-sizing or execution instruction.
