## Where VowSig currently stands

VowSig is commercially less mature than the compared platforms but has a
stronger evidence-integrity design in several areas.

Current differentiators to preserve:

- structured BUY / SELL / NO_TRADE decisions;
- reasons shown beside the decision;
- deterministic publication/risk controls;
- explicit issuance/lifecycle state;
- immutable hash-linked provenance/history;
- visible abstentions and adverse outcomes;
- historical evidence separated from forward evidence;
- frozen runtime/model/protocol identity;
- research-beta/shadow status stated explicitly;
- broker execution disabled rather than implied.

Do not trade these properties away to imitate competitor UX or marketing.

## Competitor business-model conclusions

### TradingTop

Observed as the strongest product/commercial comparator:

- AI signals and reasoning;
- model marketplace;
- signal/history views;
- subscriptions and TOP credits;
- referral/agency mechanics;
- broker/account-linked and execution-related surfaces.

Use it to understand commercial packaging, not as a checklist to copy.

### Tradebise

Observed as a search-led acquisition and monetization funnel:

`market/editorial discovery -> free analysis/signals -> registration/quota -> ads -> paid alerts/Bot`

Observed 30-day offers:

- Standard: $14.99;
- Alerts: $19.99;
- Alerts + Bot: $29.99.

Observed advertising:

- two labelled terminal/home ad regions;
- provider/self-promotional placements;
- featured-coin promotional overlay;
- Advertise/promotion surfaces for banner/native/Hot Coins placements.

Actual traffic, CPM/CPC, ad revenue, conversion and sponsorship independence
were not established.

Important SEO/product anti-patterns observed:

- symbol pages canonicalizing to homepage;
- drifting price/coverage copy;
- unreconciled performance Dataset/headline figures;
- schema/metadata inconsistencies;
- ad/overlay interference with analysis;
- route/search state mismatches.

### WaqarZaka

Observed as a low-price membership/content/community product:

- $10/month recurring content membership;
- Futures/Crypto/Stock & Commodities trade feeds;
- market updates;
- education;
- live sessions;
- Liquidity Radar/order-flow tooling;
- separate exchange-referral/community funnel.

Substantial content appears manually/discretionarily authored or managed.
The production signal-generation backend remains unknown. The inspected
evidence does not establish a reproducible AI model or independently audited
aggregate profitability.

## Product decisions

### Preserve / adopt

- complete structured signal fields and lifecycle clarity;
- reasoning beside the decision;
- immutable provenance/history;
- visible NO_TRADE and losing/adverse outcomes;
- supported-market URLs and accurate internal linking;
- factual methodology/risk explanations;
- explicit distinction between observed outcome, open state and realized
  performance.

### Test with real users

- alert channels and delivery preferences;
- subscription willingness to pay;
- educational content users actually need;
- contextual CTA from education into product use;
- whether users value broader markets versus deeper reasoning/evidence;
- whether a cleaner terminal is preferred over a content-heavy experience;
- whether ads are acceptable on education/content pages while core
  signal/evidence pages remain ad-free.

### Defer

- broad search/watchlist/trending terminal expansion;
- thousands of markets;
- multi-timeframe breadth beyond current frozen scope;
- calculators/ancillary tools;
- liquidity-score products without calibration evidence;
- TOP-like credit economies.

### Reject for current v1

- broker/automatic execution;
- deposit-linked exchange community access;
- paid ranking/urgency mechanics;
- dense advertising adjacent to live signal/evidence interpretation;
- unsupported performance/win-rate claims.

## Monetization direction

Primary hypothesis:

1. validate recurring customer value;
2. test paid beta/subscription;
3. add advertising as a secondary organic-traffic monetization layer if it
   does not reduce trust or conversion;
4. consider sponsorship/referral/affiliate economics later under explicit
   disclosure and separate controls.

Advertising should not be placed so that users could confuse sponsored content
with a VowSig BUY/SELL/NO_TRADE decision.

Preferred conceptual funnel:

`search -> useful market/education content -> optional clearly separated ads -> VowSig evidence/product -> subscription`

No ad network, sponsorship, affiliate system or paid plan is activated by this
memo.

## Pricing interpretation

Competitor price context observed:

- WaqarZaka: $10/month membership;
- Tradebise: $14.99 / $19.99 / $29.99 per 30 days;
- TradingTop: observed entry paid tier around $69/month plus larger
  quarterly/annual/lifetime/credit packages.

For VowSig, $25–$100/month remains a testable hypothesis, not a validated
price.

Working interpretation:

- $25–$35/month may be plausible for a narrow early paid beta;
- $49–$69/month may be plausible if users repeatedly value alerts,
  reasoning/history/evidence and supported-market coverage;
- around $99/month should require materially stronger recurring value.

Actual paid conversion and retention are stronger evidence than stated
willingness to pay.

Do not sell VowSig on a claim that subscribing will help users make money until
appropriate evidence exists.

## Pre-Issue #9 product decision

No major production feature expansion should be inserted before the first Issue
#9 cohort.

Reason:

- the current production beta already exposes structured AI decisions, reasons,
  immutable history/evidence and the customer-discovery form;
- the next unknown is customer value/comprehension, not another engineering
  capability;
- adding alerts, pricing, ads, accounts, broader markets or community features
  before observing real users would reduce the value of the cohort by solving
  assumed rather than observed problems.

Before outreach, allow only a narrow cohort-readiness verification:

- current production loads correctly;
- supported markets and AI evidence are visible;
- feedback submission still persists;
- no stale/broken CTA or obvious blocker prevents a first-time user from
  completing the review.

Do not treat this readiness check as a new product phase or public-surface
expansion.

### Community/live-session direction

WaqarZaka's community/live-session/personal-authority model is **out of scope**
for VowSig's current direction.

TradingTop is the more relevant product/business comparator **for VowSig's
software-led goals**, not an assertion that TradingTop is objectively superior
in every dimension. WaqarZaka remains useful evidence for membership,
education, analyst publishing and referral economics, but VowSig should not
prioritize founder-personality, live-room or community-host mechanics.

### Free-with-ads / paid-ad-free hypothesis

Owner direction:

- a future Free tier may display clearly labelled advertising;
- a future Paid tier should be ad-free;
- ads may appear on free terminal/content surfaces if they remain visually and
  semantically separate from VowSig's trading decision;
- never insert an ad inside a BUY/SELL/NO_TRADE card or between direction,
  entry, stop, target, reasoning, lifecycle or provenance fields;
- ads must not visually resemble a VowSig signal, system alert or market
  recommendation;
- start with low ad density and measure whether ads reduce product use,
  feedback completion or later paid conversion;
- educational/SEO pages remain the lowest-risk first ad surface once meaningful
  traffic exists.

This is a monetization architecture decision, not authorization to deploy an ad
network now. Actual ad implementation remains downstream of initial customer
evidence and meaningful traffic.

## Immediate next execution track — Issue #9

While Phase 8A indexing observation runs, the active growth task is the first
real-user G4 cohort.

Use the cohort to answer:

- Can users distinguish BUY/SELL/NO_TRADE from general market outlook?
- Do issuance, expiry, OPEN/CLOSED and outcome states make sense?
- Does immutable history/provenance increase trust?
- Which creates more value: alerts, more markets, deeper reasoning, education
  or historical evidence?
- Would Telegram/browser/email alerts materially increase recurring use?
- What price would users actually consider paying, and which exact capability
  makes that price worthwhile?
- Do users want educational content or primarily a clean terminal?
- Are ads acceptable on educational pages if core signal/evidence pages remain
  ad-free?

The first cohort is expected to produce product/customer evidence, not a G4
pass by itself.
