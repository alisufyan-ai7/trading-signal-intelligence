# Hypotheses Register

## H001 — Verified transparency is a differentiator

Traders will value an immutable signal history with losses visible more than an opaque headline win-rate claim.

**Test:** Compare beta engagement/conversion for users exposed to a transparent ledger and signal replay versus a simplified signal-only experience.

## H002 — Explanations improve usefulness

Quantitative signals accompanied by concise explanations/invalidation/risk context produce higher retention than unexplained BUY/SELL alerts.

**Test:** Beta cohort usage, return rate, alert interaction and interview feedback.

## H003 — Fewer high-quality signals beat high frequency

A filtered set of high-score setups will produce better customer retention than a high-volume feed of marginal setups.

**Test:** Controlled thresholds during beta after score calibration exists.

## H004 — Simple strategy families can produce robust candidates

At least one of the initial interpretable strategy families can demonstrate positive out-of-sample expectancy after realistic execution costs and robustness testing.

**Test:** Phase 0B/1 research protocol.

## H005 — AI adds more value downstream than as the raw signal generator

AI explanation/context/search over deterministic signals will be more reproducible, cheaper and more trusted than asking an LLM to invent directional trades from scratch.

**Test:** Product/user research after the quantitative baseline exists; compare explanation usefulness and consistency, not speculative directional model output.
