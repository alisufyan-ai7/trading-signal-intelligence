# S01 Trend Pullback — v0.1

**Status:** IDEA  
**Created:** 2026-09-16

## Hypothesis

When a market is in an established higher-timeframe trend, a controlled pullback toward a medium-term trend/structure area followed by renewed lower-timeframe momentum has positive continuation expectancy after realistic costs.

This version is intentionally simple. It exists to establish a reproducible baseline, not to maximize backtest performance.

## Intended regime

Expected to work best in persistent directional regimes with normal-to-moderately elevated volatility.

Expected to struggle in:

- range-bound/choppy markets
- abrupt macro-event reversals
- very high spread/illiquid periods
- mature/exhausted trends

## Baseline features

These are starting definitions and must be frozen before the first baseline experiment.

1. **H4 trend direction:** EMA(50) above EMA(200) for long; below for short.
2. **H4 trend slope:** EMA(50) direction over a fixed lookback must agree with trend direction.
3. **H1 pullback:** price returns toward H1 EMA(20) / recent breakout structure without invalidating H4 trend.
4. **H1 momentum re-entry:** closed H1 candle resumes in trend direction and closes beyond the previous candle's trend-side extreme.
5. **Volatility:** ATR(14) used for normalized stop/filters, not as a directional feature.

The exact tolerance around the pullback area and slope lookback are research parameters and must be specified before the first run.

## Entry

Evaluate only on a **closed H1 candle** in v0.1.

Long baseline concept:

- H4 trend is bullish.
- H1 has pulled back toward the predefined pullback area.
- No H4 trend invalidation has occurred.
- A closed H1 candle resumes upward momentum according to the frozen momentum rule.
- Enter at the next-bar open plus execution-cost model.

Short is symmetric.

## Stop / invalidation

Baseline candidates to test separately rather than blend opportunistically:

- structural stop beyond the pullback swing, or
- fixed ATR multiple.

One method must be selected for a given experiment version.

## Exit

Baseline experiments should test a small predeclared set, for example:

- fixed 2R target,
- fixed 3R target,
- partial at 1R + trailing remainder (only after simpler fixed-R baselines).

No discretionary exit rules.

## Costs / execution

- use source spread when available
- otherwise apply conservative per-market spread assumptions documented in experiment config
- include commission where relevant
- add a configurable slippage buffer
- do not assume fills inside a candle unless tick/intrabar sequencing supports it

## Position sizing

Research results reported primarily in R-multiples. Risk sizing is separated from the alpha test.

## Evaluation protocol

- multi-year sample where source history allows
- explicit train/validation/test segmentation
- walk-forward windows after baseline implementation
- parameter perturbation around any selected setting
- per-market and pooled results
- regime/month/year breakdown
- reject if profitability depends on a narrow parameter spike or one instrument/year

## Decision rule

v0.1 becomes `CANDIDATE` only if it demonstrates positive out-of-sample expectancy after costs with acceptable drawdown and reasonably stable neighboring parameter values. A high in-sample win rate is insufficient.
