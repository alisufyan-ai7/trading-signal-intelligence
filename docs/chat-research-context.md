## Purpose

This file preserves durable context from the current ChatGPT project conversation so research can resume without relying on long chat history or UI stream recovery.

The repository remains the source of truth. This file records conversation-derived requirements and exploratory findings that have not necessarily been promoted into formal strategy specifications.

## User objective

The working research objective is to investigate whether a multi-strategy, multi-asset trading engine can produce a relatively consistent daily profit distribution from a small starting account.

Current working targets:

- starting equity reference: about USD 500;
- desired daily profit stop: about USD 150–200;
- desired successful-trade profit unit: about USD 50;
- intended normal trade count: about 3–4 qualified trades per day, but never force trades;
- low-output day definition: net daily P&L <= USD 50;
- research preference: low-output days should ideally be no more than about 20% of trading days;
- normal daily loss stop: about USD 40;
- absolute/rare hard daily stop: about USD 60;
- no martingale;
- no increasing size after losses;
- no forced recovery trades.

These are research targets, not promises. Any strategy must be judged by out-of-sample evidence and drawdown, not by whether it can be tuned to hit the targets in-sample.

## Target-first framing

For XAUUSD, the user's intuitive reference trade is approximately 0.10 lot and a USD 5 favorable Gold move, which under a common 100-oz/lot convention is approximately USD 50 gross P&L. Broker-specific contract specifications must be verified before execution modeling.

The core research label is therefore not merely "was direction correct?" but:

> Did the market reach the required favorable target before reaching structural invalidation?

For Gold, the first target-first label tested was approximately +/- USD 5 from entry.

For other markets, the equivalent target should be normalized by tick value/contract size to a desired dollar profit unit rather than using a literal USD 5 price move.

## Badar Tanveer reverse-engineering context

The conversation began by analyzing Badar Tanveer Facebook/YouTube trading examples and uploaded videos. Directly observed recurring concepts included:

- XAUUSD focus;
- lower-timeframe execution;
- liquidity sweep;
- internal MSS / market structure shift;
- displacement;
- FVG / imbalance;
- retracement entry;
- structural stop;
- variable reward/risk rather than a universal fixed 1:9 target;
- partial profit taking;
- session/news awareness.

One uploaded Facebook clip explicitly demonstrated the sequence:

liquidity sweep -> MSS -> displacement/FVG -> retracement -> short.

This is treated as direct video evidence for the sequence, not proof of an exact proprietary rule set.

The promotional claim that 1:9 risk/reward implies a 90% win probability is mathematically incorrect and is not used in the research model.

## First strategy-level XAU backtest

Baseline data source:

- Dukascopy XAUUSD 1-minute historical data from the public Market-Data-Lab archive;
- March 1, 2026 through August 20, 2026;
- approximately 230,813 one-minute bars processed;
- broker-specific spread/commission/slippage excluded from this Phase-1 strategy screen.

Primary Engine A practical variant:

- 5-minute swing liquidity;
- sweep and close back inside;
- 1-minute MSS close;
- displacement >= roughly 1.6x recent average candle body;
- FVG;
- 50% FVG entry;
- tight FVG/structural stop;
- London/New York-oriented 06:00–18:00 UTC window;
- USD 5 favorable target.

Observed exploratory results:

- about 372 signals/trades in the six-month sample;
- full USD 5 target-first hit rate about 29.6% overall;
- March–May development target-first hit rate about 31.9%;
- June–August holdout target-first hit rate about 27.3%;
- average structural risk distance roughly USD 1.02 in Gold price;
- median favorable excursion roughly USD 3.30;
- average price expectancy around +USD 0.79 Gold/trade before execution costs.

Changing only the favorable target while keeping entries/stops approximately unchanged produced, on the later holdout sample:

- USD 2 target: about 48.7% target-first;
- USD 3 target: about 41.7%;
- USD 4 target: about 31.6%;
- USD 5 target: about 27.3%.

Interpretation: the typical favorable excursion of this setup family is closer to USD 3 than USD 5. Engine A may have positive expectancy, but it does not by itself satisfy the desired daily-income consistency target.

## Daily-output conclusion from Engine A

Under an exploratory daily simulator using approximately 0.10-lot-style Gold economics, a maximum of four trades, a soft daily stop around -USD 40 and a profit stop around +USD 150:

- average daily profit was far below USD 150;
- <= USD 50 days dominated;
- >= USD 100 days were rare;
- no meaningful frequency of USD 150+ days was achieved.

Conclusion:

> Engine A should be treated as one candidate generator, not as the whole system.

## Timeframe interpretation

"15-minute context" never meant one previous candle.

Context means a rolling structural map plus persistent levels.

Initial exploratory ranges discussed:

- 15m context: roughly 32–64 recent candles plus persistent structural/session levels;
- 5m structure: roughly 36–72 recent candles;
- 3m/1m execution: enough recent candles to identify internal structure, MSS, displacement, and retracement.

Important persistent levels may remain relevant even beyond the rolling lookback:

- previous-day high/low;
- Asian session high/low;
- London high/low;
- New York high/low;
- major swing highs/lows;
- equal highs/lows;
- unfilled FVGs;
- important order blocks.

The exact timeframe combination must be selected by data rather than assumption.

## Evidence discipline

Maintain three labels:

- OBSERVED: directly seen in uploaded/video/chart evidence or measured in a recorded experiment;
- REPEATED PATTERN: observed across multiple examples;
- HYPOTHESIS: plausible rule not yet validated.

Do not promote a strategy because of one attractive backtest. Require development/validation/final splits, cost stress, sensitivity checks, and forward evidence.
