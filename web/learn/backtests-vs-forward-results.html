Historical backtests vs forward results

Historical research can test a hypothesis. Forward evidence shows what happened after a protocol was frozen and activated.

Historical research

Backtests apply a fixed strategy or protocol to historical data. They are useful for research but remain exposed to historical selection, regime dependence and modeling assumptions.

Forward evidence

Forward evidence begins only after a protocol is frozen and activated. New decisions and outcomes then accumulate without rewriting the past.

Why VowSig shows both

The product keeps historical research and forward records visibly separate so users can see which claims come from past data and which observations occurred after activation.

Historical strength is not a guarantee of future performance, and early forward results are not automatically evidence of a durable edge.
