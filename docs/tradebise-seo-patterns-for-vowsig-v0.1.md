# Tradebise SEO patterns for VowSig v0.1 — evidence memo

Date: 2026-10-04 Asia/Karachi. Proposal only: no website/content/CTA/runtime implementation. Sources and limits: [Tradebise audit](tradebise-authenticated-product-business-seo-audit-2026-10-04.md) and [public evidence index](../research/competitors/tradebise/2026-10-04/public-evidence.json). Its cloud UI/screenshots, canonical/schema/robots/sitemap and search outcomes remain unverified. No SEO-success or ranking claim follows from page architecture.

## Observable patterns

| Pattern | First-party support | Transferable principle |
|---|---|---|
| Market-specific URLs and headings | Ten /signals/{symbol} pages; [BTC example](https://tradebise.com/signals/btcusdt) | A useful supported-market page can match a specific intent; symbol substitution alone is not useful differentiation |
| Descriptive education paths | [Learn hub](https://tradebise.com/learn), 15 article URLs | Organize explanations around a real question and link them into the product |
| Factual author/date/reading context | [Stop article](https://tradebise.com/learn/how-to-set-a-stop-loss) | Make authorship and update scope clear without manufactured authority |
| Contextual cross-links | [Structure article](https://tradebise.com/learn/reading-market-structure); 34 article cross-links resolved | Link related explanations where they help the current task |
| Education-to-product CTA | [Sizing article](https://tradebise.com/learn/position-sizing) | Offer a relevant next step after the explanation; test comprehension/value first |
| Shared site/legal navigation | [About](https://tradebise.com/about) and public information pages | Keep methodology, risk and operating identity discoverable |

Five observed editorial topic labels are getting started, risk, technical analysis, AI/technology and psychology. They are labels in the retrieved catalog; separate category routes were not found. Article sectioning and contextual links are established, but canonical/schema and a dedicated related-card module are unknown. Public app text can expose hidden dialogs; it is not evidence those states are indexed or visible to visitors.

## Apply within current VowSig governance

Repository main 099f8e89599023769ed29aa023c7916d4560ce10 and Issues [8](https://github.com/alisufyan-ai7/trading-signal-intelligence/issues/8)/[9](https://github.com/alisufyan-ai7/trading-signal-intelligence/issues/9) govern decisions. Phase 8A technical metadata/canonical/robots/sitemap/linking plus brand/performance/mobile/a11y are already live; Google/Bing submissions and Day-0 baseline are complete. Preserve that foundation and observe Day-7/14/28 results. Do not restart it as new work.

Customer-language-dependent content, CTAs, alert-channel pages and pricing conversion remain **Phase 8B, gated on initial Issue #9 aggregate real-user evidence**. AI-v1.1 protocol, model, features, guardrails, markets, lifecycle, costs and cadence remain frozen; execution disabled. Reasons/provenance/immutable history already exist and should anchor explanations.

Potential questions to validate with that evidence (not approved page titles): how to interpret NO_TRADE; when an issued setup becomes stale; what historical versus forward evidence means; how deterministic risk rules constrain an AI assessment; what the current candidate-validation status permits users to conclude. Choose 5–10 topics only after actual recurring questions and supported-market relevance are reviewed.

For each proposed page, require a supported intent, accurate product/evidence scope, meaningful unique content, appropriate metadata/canonical/index policy, useful contextual links, honest update date and one relevant next step. Evaluate impressions/indexation alongside comprehension, useful beta visits and feedback; avoid treating traffic or article count as customer value. Pricing needs actual demand/paid-conversion evidence, not another site's allowance.

Reject a mass symbol-page farm, duplicated generic copy, uncalibrated win-rate SEO, fabricated reviews/urgency, guaranteed outcomes, paid-ranking-as-analysis or raw broker-data redistribution. Do not copy competitor prose. Current evidence supports an architecture hypothesis and a small gated research backlog, not proven SEO effectiveness or permission to publish.
